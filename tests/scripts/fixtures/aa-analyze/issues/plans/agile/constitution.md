# Engineering constitution

Version: 1.0.0

### Article 1 — Simplicity first (MUST)

Prefer the smallest design that satisfies the spec.

### Article 2 — Test-first (MUST)

Every requirement has a failing test before its implementation.

### Article 3 — Library-first (SHOULD)

New capabilities start as a reusable module.
